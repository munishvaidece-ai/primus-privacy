ARCHITECTURE

One core platform, four layers, configured per organisation

Everything an organisation sees is assembled from the same core. Sector, applicability and policy live in configuration, so a new client inherits every improvement made for the previous one.

  • Improvements ship once and every client inherits them automatically.
  • Four layers, cleanly separated, so you can change one without breaking another.
  • Sector and policy specifics live in configuration, not custom code.

HOW THE BLOCKS INTERACT

A single assessment, tracked end to end

STEP 1

Organisation

Is onboarded once, with discovery captured and scope approved.

STEP 2

Scoping engine

Turns applicability decisions into an assessment with the right questions and evidence types.

STEP 3

Reviewer desk

Reviews each item, raises clarifications and accepts or rejects within the agreed window.

STEP 4

Findings & remediation

Findings, risks and owned actions run off the same engagement record.

STEP 5

Command centre

Every event feeds portfolio MIS, overdue alerts and the audit trail.

MODULE DETAIL

Four modules, in detail

01FRONT OFFICE

Organisation Journey

One sign-in for the organisation, with guided discovery, assigned questions, an evidence vault and tracking of every item.

  • Organisation registration & profile
  • Guided discovery questionnaire
  • Assigned assessment questions
  • Reusable evidence vault
  • Unified status tracker
02BACK OFFICE

Reviewer Desk & Validation

Configurable review workflow with queues ordered by risk, clarification loops, ageing clocks and escalation.

  • Reviewer and engagement desks
  • Rule-based review workflow
  • Clocks and auto-escalation
  • Clarification & response loop
  • Accept or reject with reasons
03VALUE LAYER

Findings & Remediation

Severity-rated findings, a live risk register and owned remediation actions tied to the same organisation profile.

  • Findings & gaps register
  • Risk register and severity model
  • Remediation actions with due dates
  • Closure validation
  • Policy & framework version control
04COMMAND CENTRE

Governance Analytics

Portfolio dashboards for progress, open critical risk, overdue actions and maturity, ready for board reporting.

  • Portfolio command centre
  • Open risk by client
  • Maturity analytics
  • Milestones & deliverables
  • Board-ready reports & exports
