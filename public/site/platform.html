The platform

From the first upload to the final report, with a person at every decision.

Seven phases. At each one a fixed-format document goes in, the platform checks it, an AI does the reading and the drafting, and people decide what matters.

The flow

Seven phases, one thread.

Each phase hands its result to the next. Who sends what is fixed, so the audit trail is clean.

1

Engagement set up

The client says who they are. The AI works out which laws apply.

Organisation sendsResult: Sectors and business units, which decide which controls apply in Phase 3
2

Discovery

The client describes their data. The AI builds the data map and pre-fills the questionnaire.

Organisation sendsResult: The data map and the approved questionnaire, which Phase 3 uses to decide applicability
3

Applicability

The AI decides, control by control, what applies, and says why.

Primus and the AIResult: A locked scope: the controls that will be assessed
4

Assessment

The consultant's tests go in. The AI rates each control from them.

Primus team recordsResult: A first rating per control, to be firmed up by the evidence in Phase 5
5

Evidence validation

The client sends proof. The AI reads every file and says whether it supports the control.

Organisation sendsResult: Final ratings per control
6

Findings released

The AI drafts findings for weak controls. People release them to the client.

Primus and the AIResult: Released findings the client can see
7

Remediation and final report

Findings become actions. The client proves each fix. The AI checks the proof.

Organisation sendsResult: A closed-out engagement and an issued report
Two portals, one engagement

The same flow, seen from each side.

Either can show the whole journey. Uploads, approvals and releases are recorded under the name of whoever did them.

Primus workspace

For the consultants

  • A portfolio dashboard across every client, worked out from the live records.
  • The Live Run: start each AI step, see what it read, approve or reject what it proposes.
  • Per-organisation regulation mapping: which Rules bind this client, and which sector rules sit beside them.
  • Review queues for evidence, findings and reports, with release rights for the sign-off gates.
  • An audit trail of every decision and what it was based on.
Organisation portal

For the client's team

  • A live journey: download the template, send the file, see what Primus did with it.
  • "What applies to you": the DPDP provisions and sector rules for your organisation, with dates and penalty ceilings.
  • The data map, the discovery questionnaire and the evidence centre.
  • Findings, remediation actions, risks and reports once released.
  • Clarifications and tasks, so nothing is chased by email.
The AI, on a lead

It does the reading. People keep the decisions.

The engine is the platform's own rules engine, so nothing leaves the server. Every decision shows the facts it relied on and the file cell or document it came from.

Level 1

Assist

The AI prepares everything. Nothing is applied until a person approves it.

Level 2

Supervised

The AI applies routine, low-stakes work it is very sure about. People decide the rest.

Level 3

Full autopilot

The AI applies what it is reasonably sure about. People get the exceptions and the sign-off gates.

Sign-off gates, always on

Rejecting critical evidence, releasing critical findings, issuing the final report and sending breach notices stop for a named person, at every level.

Templates first

Every file goes in a fixed template. The platform checks it before the AI reads a word, and tells the sender exactly which cell to fix.

Look, do not trust

A read-only connector can scan the client's database for tables holding personal data and compare them with what the client wrote in the inventory. It keeps names and categories, never values.
