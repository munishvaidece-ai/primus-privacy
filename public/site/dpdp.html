What it asks

The provisions that decide your work.

Each card is a section of the Act. Together they are what the platform's controls test.

s.4

Grounds for processing

Personal data may be processed only for a lawful purpose, with consent or for a certain legitimate use.

s.5

Notice

A notice must accompany or precede the request for consent, saying what data, why, how to exercise rights and how to complain to the Board.

s.6

Consent

Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, and as easy to withdraw as to give.

s.7

Certain legitimate uses

Processing without consent for listed uses, such as a voluntary provision, State benefits, legal obligations, medical emergencies and employment.

s.8

General obligations of a Data Fiduciary

Accuracy, security safeguards (s.8(5)), breach intimation (s.8(6)), erasure when the purpose ends (s.8(7)), a contact point and a grievance mechanism.

s.9

Children's data

Verifiable parental consent; no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.

s.10

Significant Data Fiduciaries

A Data Protection Officer based in India, an independent data auditor and periodic impact assessments.

s.11-14

Rights and grievance redressal

Access, correction and erasure, grievance redressal, and the right to nominate.

s.16

Transfers outside India

Transfers are allowed except to countries the Government restricts by notification.

s.17

Exemptions

Exemptions for specified purposes and for notified State instrumentalities, research and certain classes such as startups.

s.33

Penalties

The Board may impose a penalty up to the amounts in the Schedule after an inquiry.

s.3

Who it covers

Processing of digital personal data within India, including personal data collected offline and then digitised, and processing outside India in connection with offering goods or services to people in India. It does not cover personal or domestic use, or data that the person made public herself or that was made public under a legal duty.

Status of the machinery

What is settled, and what is still moving.

The RulesNotified on 13 November 2025. The Board rules took effect at once; the consent-manager rule follows after 12 months and the main duties after 18.
Shortening the windowIn January 2026 MeitY consulted on cutting the 18 months to 12. Comments closed on 4 February 2026. No amending notification had been found at the last check, so 13 May 2027 stands.
Data Protection BoardIt exists in law and its Rules are in force. MeitY invited applications for the Chairperson and Members on 6 May 2026. No appointments had been reported at the last check.
Significant Data FiduciariesThe Central Government notifies them, by volume and sensitivity of data and risk to rights. None had been reported at the last check, so "may apply" is the honest answer for large processors.
