THE SHIFT

From people doing the work to people overseeing it

The workflow stays the same: seven phases, one record. What changes is who does each step.

Before: manual workflow

  • People read every document and every screenshot.
  • People type answers, chase owners and write findings.
  • More data means more reviewers and more weeks.
  • Quality depends on who reviews it that day.

Now: AI-run workflow

  • AI agents read, match, decide, draft and chase, in parallel.
  • People see only the exceptions and the sign-off gates.
  • More data means more compute, not more waiting.
  • The same rules are applied to every item, and each one is logged.
HANDLING LARGE VOLUMES

How it copes when an organisation has a lot of data

There are two kinds of volume: the client's own data and the paperwork about it. They are handled differently.

01THE CLIENT'S DATA

Scan inside, summarise outside

Scanning runs read-only inside the client's environment. Only metadata leaves: which systems, which categories, how many records, where they flow.

  • Most sensitive systems are scanned first, then the rest
  • Later scans only look at what changed
  • No copy of personal data is stored in the platform
02THE PAPERWORK

Read everything, review the exceptions

Evidence is read by many agents at once. Duplicates are removed, and each item gets a decision, a confidence level and a cited passage.

  • Clear items are completed automatically
  • Unsure or high-risk items go to the supervisor
  • A random sample of automatic decisions is audited
03THE ONGOING WORK

Always on, not once a year

Rights requests, consent logs, vendor files and incident alerts are watched continuously, so work does not pile up between assessments.

  • Deadlines tracked automatically
  • Withdrawals honoured automatically
  • Drift flagged as it happens

HOW MUCH SUPERVISION

Three autopilot levels, set per client

Start cautiously and turn the dial up as the AI proves itself on that client's own data.

1

Assist

The AI prepares everything. A person approves each item.

Every item reaches the supervisor
2

Supervised autopilot

The AI completes items it is sure about. A person sees the rest and a random sample.

Most items complete automatically
3

Full autopilot

The AI completes everything except low-confidence items and the sign-off gates.

Only exceptions reach the supervisor

How an item is routed

Confident, low riskCompleted automatically
Confident, high riskCompleted, then sampled or sent through a gate
Unsure, low riskSampled by the supervisor
Unsure, high riskSupervisor decides

Four sign-off gates

Some actions have legal or contractual consequences, so a named person should own them. They are on by default. Each can be switched off per client.

GUARDRAILS

A privacy platform has to practise what it advises

Data stays with the client

Personal data is not copied into the platform. The AI works on metadata and masked excerpts.

Hosted in India, if you need it

Model hosting can be set to an Indian region or inside the client's own environment, which matters for government clients.

Every decision can be explained

Each decision carries the passage it relied on, a confidence level and a timestamp, so a reviewer or regulator can follow it.

Grounded in the regulatory library

Legal conclusions are drawn from the rules loaded in the platform, not from the model's memory.

Proven before it is trusted

Before a client moves up an autopilot level, the AI is tested against cases with known answers.

Pause and override at any time

One switch pauses every agent. Each override is recorded and added to the AI's review set.
