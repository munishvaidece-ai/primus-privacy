THE BLOCKS

Four modules that interlock into one privacy platform

01ENGAGE & SCOPE

Discovery & Applicability

Onboard the organisation, run discovery and decide which obligations apply before a single question is asked.

  • Organisations, engagements and stakeholders
  • Discovery & scoping
  • Applicability decisions with approvals
  • Assessment generation from scope
02ASSESS & REMEDIATE

Assessment & Evidence

Assigned questions, evidence upload, clarification loops and a validated findings register that flows into remediation.

  • Assessment centre and question bank
  • Evidence review with accept or reject
  • Findings & gaps, risk register
  • Remediation oversight and closure validation
03OPERATE

Privacy Operations

The day-to-day registers and workflows that keep a programme running between assessments.

  • Processing activities and data inventory
  • Data principal rights
  • Consent & notices
  • Breach management, third-party risk, policies & controls
04INTELLIGENCE

Reports & Analytics

Regulatory knowledge and maturity analytics that turn a set of findings into a story leadership can act on.

  • Regulatory library
  • Applicability matrix and sector frameworks
  • Maturity analytics
  • Reports & exports, audit trail
WHY TEAMS ADOPT IT

Built around what a privacy lead is measured on

One record, every stakeholder

Scope, answers, evidence, findings and actions share one engagement record, so nobody reconciles spreadsheets before a review.

Evidence that stands up

Each finding points to the evidence behind it, and every approval, rejection and release is written to the audit trail.

Remediation that closes

Findings become owned actions with due dates. Overdue items surface on the command centre, and closure needs validation.

Reporting without the rework

Reports and exports are generated from validated findings, so the board pack matches what the team signed off.
